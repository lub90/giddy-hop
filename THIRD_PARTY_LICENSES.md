## Only used to build and test

Vite, vite-plugin-pwa, vite-plugin-singlefile, TypeScript, Vitest, Playwright,
fake-indexeddb and their dependencies (MIT, Apache-2.0, ISC and similar). They
are not part of the published game.

## Sounds and pictures

All built-in sounds are generated by the game's own code; all pictures (horses,
icons, courses) are drawn by the game's own code. Neither the repository nor the
published game contains sound or image files of other authors. Own recordings
placed in the git-ignored folder `local-assets/` or uploaded in the game stay on
the user's device.
